# Virtual Productivity Pet
A mobile application that turns personal productivity into a fun, rewarding experience by combining a structured habit tracker with a virtual pet simulation. Instead of just ticking off checkboxes, users nurture a digital pet whose growth and happiness depend directly on their real-world consistency.

---

## Features

* **Flexible Task Management:** Create, organize, and track habits and to-dos on a weekly and monthly basis.
* **Virtual Pet Simulation:** Gain experience points (XP), and interact with Virtual Pet by completing your scheduled tasks.
* **Dynamic Evolution:** Watch your pet grow, evolve, and unlock new stages based on your daily and weekly productivity streaks.
* **Engaging UX:** Designed to keep motivation high by turning daily routines into a gamified journey.

---

## Tech Stack

* **Frontend/Mobile:** React Native, Axios, TansQuery, TanStack Query
* **Backend / Database:** Hapi.js, Prisma, Postgres, Joi, Typescript
